## How to read this

- **precision@5** is the share of the five recommended reviewers who are in the right field and genuinely conflict-free. The random baseline is what you'd get picking names from a hat.
- **COI leaks** must be zero. Anything else is a product-killing bug.
- **Gini** is 0 when every reviewer gets the same number of invites and 1 when one person gets them all.
- **ledger_total** must be zero (double-entry). **escrow_unsettled** must be zero after the expiry sweep.
- **spend_reconciles**: what publishers spent equals what reviewers got plus what the platform kept.
